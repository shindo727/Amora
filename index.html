Common Questions

How do I confirm my booking?
Just message us on WhatsApp with your preferred date and package. We'll confirm within a few hours and send you all the details.
Is the experience suitable for all ages?
Yes, the cable car is comfortable and safe for adults of all ages. Children are welcome when accompanied by adults.
Can I add a personal touch (proposal, anniversary)?
Absolutely — we love helping couples create unforgettable memories. Tell us in advance and we'll arrange something special.
What if the weather is bad?
Sharm El Sheikh has over 360 sunny days a year, but if conditions aren't ideal, we'll reschedule at no extra cost.
Do you offer hotel pickup?
Hotel pickup is included in the VIP Package. For other packages, we can arrange transport — just ask us.
